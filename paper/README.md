# Automation, Capital Taxation, and Public Insurance

Nathan Barnard, September 2026.

This is the current long-form working-paper set. The 97-page main paper develops the
economic argument from historical motivation through the Brownian Ramsey problem,
payoff span, capital-tax dynamics, event-arrival economies, and a fixed-policy
transition account. Five separate appendices provide 101 pages of supporting material.
